Hyphenation patterns for American English
=========================================

The files in this directory are the en_US TeX hyphenation patterns from
the hyph-utf8 project, vendored verbatim from the upstream texlive
distribution at:

  texlive/texmf-dist/tex/generic/hyph-utf8/patterns/txt/

Files
-----

  hyph-en-us.pat.txt   4938 hyphenation patterns (Knuth-Liang format)
  hyph-en-us.hyp.txt     14 exception words
